# Brand and copy rules for account white papers

## Visual
- Headings, eyebrows, pills, stats and step numbers: Satoshi, static Bold (700) and Black (900). Body, captions, footnotes: Inter Regular (400) and Medium (500). Never the variable fonts: Chrome prints them as Type 3, which breaks copy, search and screen readers. Check with pdffonts: only Satoshi-* and Inter-* names, all embedded, none Type 3.
- Eyebrow and pill letter-spacing at most 0.05em. Wider tracking extracts as "SECURIT Y" or "H OW I T WO R KS".
- Colours: Primary Blue #1882FF, Hero Blue #0B51AE, Body Text #3A3A3A, Dark Navy #0C2235, White. Borders #E5E9F1 / #E3E8F0.
- Flag swatches, in this order: red #e5484d, green #2fa36b, yellow #f5c518, blue #1882ff, purple #8e4ec6. Never orange or teal.
- Logo: the SVGs in template/assets (blue mark + dark text on light; white on navy or blue). Never re-typeset the wordmark.
- Letter size (8.5 x 11 in), 4 to 6 pages, page numbers bottom right in tabular numerals.
- Every mockup is labelled "(illustrative)" or "Illustrative ..." and uses reserved example domains (vendor.example).

## Copy (core humanizer rules; if you have a house-style skill, run it too)
- No em dashes. Use commas, colons, full stops or "to" in ranges.
- No significance inflation ("pivotal", "landscape", "testament"), no stacked praise ("powerful, seamless"), no banned words: robust, seamless, unlock, delve, leverage, cutting-edge, game-changing.
- No "-ing" benefit glosses (", enabling...", "This helps...") and no "not X, but Y" negative parallelisms. Name the actor, the object and the visible result.
- Avoid rule-of-three headlines and two-beat staccato ("X is publishing. AI is reading."). One clear sentence.
- Plain verbs, short sentences, second person ("your team"). Customer facts in their words and attributed to them.
- Headline per page states the outcome for this account, not a feature name.
- Spell out what a number counts (pages vs domains vs monitors) every time.
